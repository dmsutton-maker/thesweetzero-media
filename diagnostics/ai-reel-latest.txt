Dinner doesn't have to be complicated to be good for you.

**Sheet-Pan Lemon Garlic Chicken + Veggies**
- 4 chicken thighs
- 2 cups broccoli or green beans
- 1 lemon, sliced
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- Olive oil, salt, pepper

Toss everything on one pan, roast at 400°F for 30-35 min. That's it. No added sugar, no complicated steps, just real food that fills you up.

This is the kind of meal you can make on a Thursday when you're tired and still want something that feels like it took effort (even though it didn't).
